ATEEZ

about

ATEEZ (에이티즈) is an 8-member K-Pop group formed by KQ Entertainment. Renowned for their intense choreography, captivating storyline, and high-energy performances, ATEEZ has captured global attention since their debut in October 2018. They were also the first K-pop boy group to perform in Coachella 2024.
